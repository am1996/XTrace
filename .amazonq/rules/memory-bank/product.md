# Product Overview

## Project Purpose
XTRACE is an intelligent, compliant track and trace software solution engineered for organizations operating within stringent regulatory environments such as pharmaceuticals, biotech, and medical devices. It provides end-to-end supply chain visibility while ensuring adherence to the highest standards of data integrity and interoperability.

## Core Value Proposition
XTRACE delivers a unified platform that tracks the physical movement of goods while ensuring absolute integrity and compliance of associated electronic records through:
- **21 CFR Part 11 Compliance**: FDA-compliant electronic records and electronic signatures
- **EPCIS 2.0 Standard**: GS1-compliant supply chain event capture and sharing
- **Regulatory Readiness**: Built-in support for DSCSA and international traceability mandates

## Key Features

### 21 CFR Part 11 Compliance
- **Secure Electronic Signatures**: Dual-factor authentication with permanent linkage to records including signer identity, timestamp, and signature meaning
- **Comprehensive Audit Trails**: Automatic generation of secure, time-stamped audit trails recording all create, modify, and delete operations
- **User Access Controls**: Role-based permissions limiting system access to authorized individuals only
- **System Validation Support**: Documentation structure supporting IQ/OQ/PQ validation processes

### EPCIS 2.0 Compliance
- **Standardized Data Exchange**: Captures "What, When, Where, and Why" of product movements using globally recognized formats
- **Enhanced Interoperability**: Seamless integration with ERP, WMS, and trading partner systems
- **End-to-End Traceability**: Real-time visibility into chain of custody from raw material to patient
- **Multiple Format Support**: JSON-LD and XML output formats for maximum compatibility

### Core Capabilities
- **Product Management**: Track products with GS1-compliant identifiers
- **Batch Management**: Manage production batches with full traceability
- **Serial Number Management**: Generate and track serialized items with pooling support
- **Equipment Tracking**: Monitor equipment used in production processes
- **Storage Location Management**: Track inventory across storage locations
- **EPCIS Event Generation**: Create compliant ObjectEvent, AggregationEvent, TransactionEvent, TransformationEvent, and AssociationEvent records

## Target Users
- **Pharmaceutical Manufacturers**: Companies requiring FDA compliance and supply chain traceability
- **Biotech Organizations**: Firms needing rigorous data integrity and audit capabilities
- **Medical Device Companies**: Manufacturers subject to regulatory traceability requirements
- **Supply Chain Managers**: Professionals managing regulated product distribution
- **Quality Assurance Teams**: Personnel responsible for compliance and validation
- **Regulatory Affairs**: Teams preparing for audits and inspections

## Use Cases
- **Product Serialization**: Generate and manage unique serial numbers for individual units
- **Supply Chain Event Tracking**: Record receiving, shipping, and transformation events
- **Regulatory Compliance**: Maintain audit trails and electronic signatures for FDA inspections
- **Recall Management**: Quickly identify and trace affected products through the supply chain
- **Trading Partner Integration**: Share EPCIS-compliant data with distributors and healthcare providers
- **Anti-Counterfeiting**: Verify product authenticity through serialization and traceability
- **Validation Support**: Generate documentation for system qualification processes
